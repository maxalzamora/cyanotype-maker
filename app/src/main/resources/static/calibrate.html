Print a step wedge, expose it, photograph the print. The measurement replaces the assumed density curve with the one your printer, film and chemistry actually produce.

1 · Print the target

Print on the same transparency and at the same resolution you use for negatives. Expose it like a print, then let it dry.

21 bars from solid ink to clear film, edge to edge. No labels: the only thing you have to do with the print is crop to the stripes.

2 · Photograph the print

Crop to the bars, light it evenly, and avoid reflections. The photograph does not need to be colour-accurate — it only has to order the tones and find both ends of the range.

No darkroom to hand? The simulated scan is a photograph of a print that was never made, generated from a known curve. It exercises the whole measurement, and reports how closely the profile reproduces it.

3 · What was measured

    Use the profile →

    The measurement is relative: a phone camera cannot know absolute density. It can find paper white, find the darkest blue the print reaches, and say where each bar sits in between — which is exactly what a tone curve needs.
